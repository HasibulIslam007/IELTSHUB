Demonstration audio. Part 1. Welcome to the Riverside Garden registration desk. My name is Anna. Our garden is on Willow Street, next to the old post office. New members pay eighteen pounds for the season. The next introduction session is on Saturday at ten in the morning. Please bring gloves; we provide the tools. You can grow vegetables and flowers, but fruit trees are not permitted because their roots require too much space. Each plot measures six square metres. There is a water tap beside the wooden shed. Members must lock the main gate when they leave. To report a problem, email our coordinator, whose name is Martin. We hold our annual harvest picnic in September. The garden closes at sunset each evening.
