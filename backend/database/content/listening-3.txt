Demonstration audio. Part 3. Tutor: Tell me about your project, Mia. Student: We want to study food waste in student kitchens. We considered a questionnaire, but decided to use daily diaries instead. Tutor: How many participants? Student: Thirty volunteers from three halls of residence. Each will record discarded food for fourteen days. We will supply digital scales so they can measure its weight. Tutor: What categories will you use? Student: Fruit and vegetables, cooked meals, and bread. We will exclude liquids because measuring them consistently would be difficult. Tutor: What about privacy? Student: Diaries will use identification numbers, not names. We will store the files on the university server. Tutor: When is the pilot? Student: Next Monday, with four students. The final report is due in November. Tutor: Remember to discuss limitations. Volunteers might change their habits because they know they are being observed. Student: We will mention that in the conclusion.
