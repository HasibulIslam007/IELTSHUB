Demonstration audio. Part 2. Good morning, everyone. Welcome to the Coast and Community Museum. The building began life as a warehouse in 1890. Today our main exhibition is on the ground floor. It describes local fishing and boat building. Start in the east gallery, where you will find a restored wooden boat. Next, the west gallery contains photographs taken by island residents. Upstairs, there is a temporary exhibition about navigation. You may take photographs without flash, except in the temporary exhibition. Audio guides are available in four languages and cost three pounds. The café closes at four, one hour before the museum. Our gift shop sells books and locally made pottery. Children can join a map-making activity at two in the afternoon. Meet the workshop leader at reception. If the fire alarm sounds, leave through the north exit and meet beside the stone fountain.
